## 🚀 Core Student Tools & Modules

CampusAxis provides algorithmic utilities built specifically according to Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan and institution-specific grading benchmarks:

| Student Tool | Description | Direct Access |
|---|---|---|
| **University GPA & CGPA Calculator** | Modular grading system supporting 260+ Pakistani universities (FAST, COMSATS, NUST, UET, PU, GIKI) with custom grading scales and semester projections. | [Open GPA Calculator](https://campusaxis.pk/gpa-calculator) |
| **University Merit & Aggregate Calculator** | Algorithmic aggregate calculation for MDCAT, ECAT, NET, NAT, and university entrance criteria across engineering, medical, CS, and business programs. | [Calculate University Merit](https://campusaxis.pk/merit-calculator) |
| **50,000+ University Past Papers Archive** | Clean, searchable repository of midterm and final exam past papers organized by university, department, degree, and semester with fast preview & download. | [Explore Past Papers](https://campusaxis.pk/past-papers) |
| **University Student & LMS Portals Directory** | Direct gateway to 260+ verified Pakistani university LMS, student portals, CMS logins, and official examination boards. | [Browse Student Portals](https://campusaxis.pk/student-portals) |
| **Academic & Admissions Intelligence Blog** | Comprehensive guides on university admissions, HEC equivalency, CGPA improvement strategies, grading formula breakdowns, and scholarship alerts. | [Read Academic Guides](https://campusaxis.pk/blog) |
